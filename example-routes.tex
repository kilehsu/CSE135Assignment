\section*{Sessions}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llp{7.5cm}@{}}
\toprule
Method & Route & Description \\
\midrule
GET    & /api/sessions       & Retrieve all sessions \\
GET    & /api/sessions/\{id\}  & Retrieve a specific session \\
POST   & /api/sessions       & Create a new session \\
PUT    & /api/sessions/\{id\}  & Update a session \\
DELETE & /api/sessions/\{id\}  & Delete a session and cascading data \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\section*{Notes}
\begin{itemize}
  \item GET without an ID returns all records; with an ID returns one.
  \item POST does not take an ID (auto-generated). PUT and DELETE require an ID.
  \item Data stored in PostgreSQL. Session IDs from \texttt{sessionStorage}.
  \item Collector ingest (\texttt{POST /collect}) lives on \texttt{collector.lehum.site}, separate from these routes.
\end{itemize}
